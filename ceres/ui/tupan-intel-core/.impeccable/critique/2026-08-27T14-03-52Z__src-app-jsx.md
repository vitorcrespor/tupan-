## Design Specificity Verdict

Ceres is product-specific at the idea level: H3 geography, AVM, portfolio intelligence, agent context, and the voice orb form a credible real-estate command center. The implementation is not yet coherent as one authored system. It layers a bespoke glossy metric treatment, generic form/table primitives, fixed-color analytical modules, and a separate voice world. Product specificity is strong; system cohesion is weak.

Detector evidence supports this judgment: five motion warnings — Accordion08 elastic easing, MetricKnob and NeuRail layout transitions, and two generic bouncing loaders. It also found raw highlight colors in shared primitives and an overly broad dark-mode override that targets class-name substrings with !important. No P0 blocking problem was found.

## Overall Impression

The entry feeling is premium and intelligent; the middle of a task feels less decisive because every surface competes for attention; the end seldom presents a single commercially useful next action. The biggest opportunity is to turn the existing assets into one disciplined operating system, rather than adding further visual treatments.

## What’s Working

- The Ceres/H3/AVM/agent constellation feels genuinely specific to a broker workflow.
- Async query states are materially stronger than average across market, report, and dashboard surfaces.
- A real design-system foundation already exists: theme registry, AppShell, TopBar, Panel/KpiCard, select primitives, and navigation groups.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Theme contract leaks across the product

MarketIntelligence and Portfolio use fixed palettes, while Panel/KpiCard/MetricKnob embed white gloss/light styling. index.css then tries to repair dark mode using broad html.dark class-substring selectors. This makes dark themes fragile and explains the recurring pale/glare issues.

**Fix:** introduce semantic surface, edge, control, popover, and chart-series tokens; consume them in shared primitives; remove broad selector repairs and hard-coded light color values.

### [P1] The overview has no deliberate decision hierarchy

AI brief, KPI cards, trends, activity, portfolio diagnosis, H3 analysis, and listings are peer-weight sections in a long scroll.

**Fix:** create three operating layers: one evidence-backed “act now” decision, 3–4 market-pulse measures, then progressively disclosed exploration. Preserve the data; change the hierarchy.

### [P1] Interaction grammar is inconsistent and sometimes undiscoverable

MetricKnob’s 58×12 tab, generic selects, plus/context tabs, hover behavior, and three navigation modes do not teach one another.

**Fix:** establish one control grammar for metric/context switching, give all targets a real hit area and label, and share escape/back/navigation behavior across shell and voice mode.

### [P1] Language and mode consistency break trust

Welcome/auth use English while operating pages use Portuguese. Voice, command, and ordinary management screens look like different applications.

**Fix:** commit to pt-BR or another deliberate content language; enforce shared typography, surface, spacing, and terminology across authentication, dashboard, voice, and administration.

### [P1] Property management is visually and operationally unsafe

Data management relies on placeholder-only fields, silent console failures, and immediate icon-only delete. Similar dense controls appear in saved searches.

**Fix:** give every field a persistent label, add explicit destructive confirmation plus recovery feedback, reveal advanced fields progressively, and preserve form values after failures.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex, power user:** the Command-K affordance is decorative; no matching listener, keyboard-first navigation, bulk action path, or accelerator exists. Metric controls are click/hover designed rather than fast to operate.

**Sam, keyboard/screen-reader user:** KpiCard puts a button role around real nested controls; the metric tab is far below a practical target size; several forms and icon-only actions lack durable accessible labels.

**Jordan, first-timer:** AVM, DOM, H3, and DATA/MODEL/AI tags are unexplained. Navigation offers destinations without a suggested first path, and the overview fails to say what action should come first.

## Minor Observations

- Voice toolbar and transcript use fixed dimensions/coordinates that are risky on small screens.
- AiBrief auto-calls the agent on mount, which can feel slow and costly before intent is expressed.
- 9–11px muted text should be checked at 200% zoom in every theme.
- App.jsx statically imports all page modules; the ~2.2 MB initial bundle undermines an otherwise polished interface.

## Questions to Consider

- If Ceres could lead with one evidence-backed commercial decision every morning, which first-viewport section would disappear?
- Is the voice orb a first-class operating mode or a branded alternate universe? What must stay identical across the boundary?
- Which three signals should carry confidence, provenance, and action—and what can be removed once they do?
